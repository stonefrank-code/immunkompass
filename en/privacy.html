2. What data does the app process?

You can enter the following information in the app yourself:

  • selected or custom immunosuppressants, brand name and target range,
  • doses with date and note,
  • blood drug levels with date,
  • other lab values (e.g. blood count, kidney, liver and inflammation values) as well as custom lab values with name, unit, date and note,
  • events such as hospital stays, illness, doctor’s appointments or additional medications with period and note,
  • times for dose reminders.

This information is health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. It is stored and processed on your devices and – if you use iCloud – synced between your devices via your private iCloud storage (see section 3). It is not transmitted to the developer. Processing takes place solely at your initiative and for your personal use.

3. iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud with your Apple Account on your device, the app additionally stores your entries in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). This makes them available on all your devices with the same Apple Account (iPhone, iPad, Mac). The data is kept in your personal iCloud storage. The developer can neither view nor retrieve it.

The service provider is Apple (for users in the EU: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). Apple’s Privacy Policy and the iCloud Terms and Conditions apply. Apple may also process the data on servers outside the EU.

You can turn off syncing at any time: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → turn off ImmunKompass. The data then remains only on the respective device.

4. Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app uses the same private iCloud database as iPhone, iPad and Mac (see section 3). It shows your entries, and you can add levels and blood values there – even without an iPhone nearby. No developer server is involved.

5. Notifications

Dose reminders are local notifications. They are scheduled and shown on your device. The app asks for your permission once. You can revoke it at any time in the iOS settings.

6. Export and Sending to Your Doctor

You can send a short summary (level, dose, target range, events) yourself by e-mail, WhatsApp or another app. The app only opens the chosen app with the prepared text; nothing is sent until you confirm it there. The terms of the chosen service apply to the transmission (e.g. your e-mail provider, WhatsApp/Meta).

If you use the CSV export, a file is created. You decide yourself via the iOS share sheet where it goes (e.g. Files, Mail, AirDrop). The terms of the chosen service then apply to further processing.

7. Backups

App data may be part of your device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer). You control these backups in the iOS settings. Apple’s privacy policy applies to them.

8. App Store

When you download the app, Apple processes data such as your Apple Account and purchase information. The developer has no influence on this. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies. Apple may provide the developer with anonymized statistics (e.g. download numbers, crash reports) – only if you have agreed to this in the iOS settings.

9. Deletion

You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. With iCloud sync turned on, they are then also deleted on your other devices and in iCloud. If you delete the app, the data on that device is removed; the copy in iCloud remains. You delete it under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → ImmunKompass.

10. Your Rights

As the developer does not receive any personal data from you, they cannot provide information about it. Apple is your point of contact for data stored in iCloud. You can still reach us with privacy questions at the e-mail address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. Changes

If the app changes, we will update this privacy policy. The version published here applies.
