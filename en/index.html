ImmunKompass

A diary for your immunosuppression

Support Privacy Terms of Use Legal Notice Deutsch

ImmunKompass helps organ transplant recipients and other immunosuppressed people keep track of doses, drug levels (e.g. tacrolimus, everolimus) and important blood values – on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Important: ImmunKompass is not a medical device. The app does not calculate doses and does not give treatment recommendations. Only change your medication after talking to your doctor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
On your devices and – if you are signed in to iCloud – in your private iCloud storage. This keeps your data the same on iPhone, iPad and Mac automatically. The developer has no access to it; there is no separate account and no developer server. The Apple Watch also syncs via iCloud and works without an iPhone nearby.

How do I know my target range?
Your doctor sets the target range for your trough level. You enter it under Settings → Medication.

How do I send my values to my doctor?
Open a medication and tap the share icon at the top. You can send the summary by e-mail, WhatsApp or another app.

What are events for?
Under Medications → Add → Add Event you can record e.g. an illness, a hospital stay or an antibiotic together with its period. In the Overview they appear below the dose; illness and hospital stays are also shaded in the charts. This lets you see whether a level or blood value falls into that time.

The Apple Watch shows “No Data”.
Check that the watch is signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account as your iPhone and has Wi-Fi or cellular. The data then arrives automatically via iCloud – add your medications on iPhone or iPad first.

How do I back up my data?
With iCloud sync turned on, it is stored in your iCloud storage and available on all your devices. It is also part of your regular device backup. In addition, you can save it as a CSV file under Settings → Export.

How do I delete everything?
Deleting the app removes the data on that device. You remove the copy in iCloud under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → ImmunKompass.
